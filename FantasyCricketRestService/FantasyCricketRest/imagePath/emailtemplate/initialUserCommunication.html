A message from your fantasy league friends: Guru, Jattin, Neeraj, Rajib and Sam

Hello Friends, 		 
 
We are excited to share some news with you.

Playing fantasy sports has been a long time passion for us, and last year a few of us took this passion a step further - we dreamed of playing IPL 2019 on our own fantasy sports app.

For the past few months, we have been working very hard to build the app that we've named fanzania. We wanted you to be the first to know that we are ready with a beta (trial) version of the app. We invite you to play IPL 2019 on fanzania - we'd love to have you onboard.

How can you get started?
We have the beta version on Android and Web. Just download the Android app or register on the Web version at www.fanzania.com to create your account. We are working hard to launch an iOS version very soon; in the interim, if you have an iPhone please try our Web version.

How can you share you feedback about fanzania?
You can reach out to one of us directly or get in touch through our Contact us page to share your feedback. We'd really appreciate your honest opinions, be it good or bad, as it will help us in improving the app for the upcoming tournaments.

What are some features the beta version of fanzania offers?
The beta version offers many of the known features that you are used to seeing in other apps. In addition, we have added some unique features such as -
  • Tournament, team and league statistics to help you bring the best our of your game
  • Live points scoring for leagues to give an exciting playing experience
  • New varieties of power-play options to boost your team's performance
  • More flexibility on transfers so you can strategize better and draft your best team
  
 
This is just a beginning. We have a lot more interesting features lined up in our upcoming releases. We have started small so we could get your early feedback on fanzania. We are really keen and excited for you to try it out. Any questions, just give us a shout on Twitter @playfanzania.

May the 4s and 6s be with you!

Cheers,
fanzania Team
