We have an important update to share with regards to the upcoming ‘Indian T20 League’ starting on 9 Apr 21.

As you might know, fanzania is run by a community of fantasy cricket fans (all the core founding members have been building it out of passion while still having other day jobs). For over two years, we have been self-funding the platform to avoid dilution of the core "cricket fantasy" spirit. We are now at a stage where in order to sustain and continually improve the platform, we need help to raise funds within our community to cover a portion of ours costs - particularly in light of the recent exponential spike in expenditure to support the growth your participation has brought to the platform. This is where we need your help to ensure we keep it a community driven platform.

For the upcoming tournament, we are introducing very nominal subscription fees. Please note that there is still a no-fee option, so you can continue to play for free. The subscription options will be as below:

FREE - 0 INR
all features excluding eligibility to win prizes and live scoring feature

PRIZE ELIGIBILITY - 99 INR
all features including eligibility to win prizes in both ‘Tournament' and ‘Daily Match Contest’ modes; excludes live scoring feature

LIVE SCORE - 99 INR
all features including live scoring; excludes eligibility to win prizes in ‘Tournament' and ‘Daily Match Contest’ modes

FULL PACKAGE - 149 INR
all features including eligibility to win prizes in both ‘Tournament' and ‘Daily Match Contest’ modes, and live scoring feature

How to subscribe?
  • Web: You can subscribe via our website www.fanzania.com (go to ‘Subscription’ page).
  • Android app: You will be able to subscribe via the Android app shortly once it has been published in the Play Store. In the meantime, you can download the same latest version from our website.
  • iOS: iOS users will need to subscribe via our website.
We will be directing 10% of the funds secured from the subscription fees towards an upcoming and needful sports foundation.
